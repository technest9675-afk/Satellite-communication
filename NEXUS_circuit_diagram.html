        Where these numbers come from. Net names, references and pin numbers follow the board netlists (NX-MB1 EM and SN, NX-TX1, NX-RX1, NX-TRK1) and the pin-list CSVs in the design package. Rail voltages, currents, timing and sensitivities follow the board calculation report and the link-budget model that ships with it — including the 36 MHz clock, the 114.3 kbit/s ground link, the 5.17 V and 3.315 V rails, and the −75.3 dBm receiver sensitivity at 1e-3 symbol error rate.

        This is a build drawing, not a manufacturing release. Before fabrication, run the netlist through your own ERC, check the laser interlock against your local laser-safety rules, and confirm the DFB pin-out with the supplier.
